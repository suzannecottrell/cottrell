Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SuZanne Cottrell ("I," "me," or "my") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit this website and use the contact or consultation request forms.

When you submit a contact form or consultation request, I collect the following information:

  • Book title and genre (if provided)

  • Any additional information you choose to share about your project

How I Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely for the following purposes:

I take the security of your personal information seriously. Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to me. I do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties.
