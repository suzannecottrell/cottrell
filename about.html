Behind the Strategy

A thoughtful approach to helping authors build visibility, clarity, and meaningful connections with readers.

A great book deserves the opportunity to reach the people who will value it.

SuZanne Cottrell is a Literary Marketing Strategist based in New York, working with authors internationally. Her work is centered on one core belief: a great book deserves the opportunity to reach the people who will value it.

The publishing world is filled with talented authors and meaningful stories, yet visibility can often be one of the greatest challenges after the writing is complete. SuZanne works at the intersection of literature, strategy, branding, and audience growth to help authors approach that challenge with greater clarity.

Her approach goes beyond simply promoting a book. She looks at the bigger picture: the author's goals, the book's positioning, the intended audience, and the opportunities available to create a stronger and more sustainable presence.

Working with authors across genres and publishing journeys, SuZanne believes that effective literary marketing should never feel like a one-size-fits-all formula. Every book has its own identity. Every author has a different story. Every audience responds differently. Strategy should reflect that.

My Mission

To help authors approach marketing with greater clarity, confidence, and purpose by developing strategies that strengthen visibility, build meaningful audience connections, and support long-term author growth.

My Vision

To contribute to a literary landscape where talented authors have access to thoughtful marketing strategies that help their work reach beyond publication and into meaningful connection with readers.

What I Believe

Every Author Has a Unique Story

Marketing should highlight what makes an author and their work distinctive.

Visibility Should Be Intentional

Being everywhere is not the same as being seen by the right people.

Strategy Creates Direction

Clear goals and thoughtful decisions are more valuable than random marketing activity.

Connection Matters

The strongest marketing creates genuine relationships between authors and readers.
